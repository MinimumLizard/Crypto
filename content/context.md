## The oil shock

The 2026 Iran war closed the Strait of Hormuz from late February and closed Bab
al-Mandab as well. Brent was around $109 in early September.

**Hormuz reopening is the highest-leverage single variable for an alt book.**
It sits at the head of the chain the `/geopolitics` page draws:

> oil → inflation → Fed → real yields / USD → liquidity → crypto

## Regulation

The CLARITY Act failed. The SEC issued an innovation exemption on 17 Sep 2026,
which favours DEXs and tokenization. US midterms are on 3 Nov 2026, and
midterm-year seasonality is a live factor in the cycle view.

## Market structure

The market has split into three regimes, and they should not be read as one:

1. **Hard money** — BTC, ZEC, XMR, gold.
2. **Cash-generating protocols** — the names where holders' revenue is real.
3. **2021-era liquidity beta** — correctly priced, not cheap.

The 2021–25 token vintage launched with low floats and huge FDVs, so a rising
market has to absorb continuous unlocks. The working assumption is **sector
rotation, not a 2021-style altseason**.

## What this means for the terminal

Prioritise, in order: the oil → liquidity transmission chain; the BTC 50W
confirmation counter; sector rotation; supply overhang; and the quality of
protocol revenue that actually reaches holders. Generic indicators come last.
